Our objectives

Turning compassion into action.

Six connected objectives guide our commitment to children, families, and caregivers.

01

Awareness

Raise awareness about sickle cell disease.

02

Early screening

Promote early screening and counseling.

03

Healthcare access

Support access to quality healthcare.

04

Emotional care

Provide psychosocial and emotional support.

05

Family empowerment

Empower families and caregivers.

06

Advocacy

Advocate for policies that protect the rights of sickle cell warriors.
